# FX Overnight Recap - 2026-09-17

Wed 16 Sep 19:00 -> Thu 17 Sep 07:00 CEST (NY afternoon -> Asia)

Overnight was a USD-positive FOMC reaction: the Fed moved the target to 4.00% from 3.75%, and even though that matched the forecast the dollar strengthened 0.52% against majors and DXY added 0.6%. SPX closed -0.148% and VIX moved up 2.7 points, keeping the tape mildly risk-off but not panicked.
