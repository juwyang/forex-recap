Where the money went

Overnight trade was anchored by an Australian CPI miss: trimmed mean CPI printed 0.2% versus 0.3% forecast, triggering a 0.48% own-currency markdown in AUD. The kiwi led the G10 with a 0.094% strength score while USD and CAD lagged at -0.110% and -0.111%. Equities firmed (SPX +0.411%) and the dollar index fell -0.14%, so the tape had a risk-on, USD-soft tone rather than a haven bid. Fed's Williams floated a late-2026 hike but the dollar still failed to gain.
