# FX Daily Recap - 2026-09-29

Mon 28 Sep 19:00 -> Tue 29 Sep 19:00 CEST (Asia -> London -> NY morning)

The day was dominated by a broad USD safe-haven bid as Iran/Russia headlines kept risk sentiment fragile; DXY gained 0.431% with SPX down 0.548% and VIX up 0.661%. JPY was second-strongest while AUD and NZD were weakest after RBA press conference comments reversed post-decision gains. US consumer confidence missed badly at 81.9 vs 89.2 but the USD basket reaction was muted, showing the bid was not data-driven.
